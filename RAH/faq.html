Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our vehicle export services

How does the process work?
We source vehicles through trusted Japanese exporters, provide a quotation, handle payment, and arrange shipping to your Caribbean port. The entire process takes 4-8 weeks depending on availability and shipping schedules.
What countries do you serve?
We serve all Caribbean nations including Barbados, Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Bahamas, Dominican Republic, and all other Caribbean islands. We also serve Guyana and Suriname.
Are the vehicles inspected?
Yes, all vehicles pass Japanese inspection and are graded (Auction Grade 4+). We provide full inspection reports and vehicle history documents.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept bank wire transfers, secure online payments, and credit options are available. All transactions are processed securely.
How long does shipping take?
Shipping from Japan to the Caribbean typically takes 4-6 weeks depending on the destination port and shipping schedules.
Can I request a specific vehicle?
Absolutely! We can search for specific makes, models, years, and specifications based on your requirements. Contact us with your preferences.
Do you handle customs clearance?
We provide guidance and support for customs clearance at the destination port. Our team assists with documentation and procedures.
What is the warranty on vehicles?
Vehicles are sold as-is, but we only source high-grade vehicles with verified condition reports. We can also assist with extended warranty options.
