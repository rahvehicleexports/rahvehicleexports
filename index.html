Frequently Asked Questions

How does the process work?
We source vehicles through trusted Japanese exporters, provide a quotation, handle payment, and arrange shipping to your Caribbean port.
What countries do you serve?
All Caribbean nations including Barbados, Jamaica, Trinidad, and more. We ship island-wide.
Are the vehicles inspected?
Yes, all vehicles pass Japanese inspection and are graded (Auction Grade 4+). We provide full reports.
What payment methods are accepted?
Bank wire transfer, secure online payment, and credit options available.
